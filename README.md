# 📊 Sales & Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Sales & Performance Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze business sales, profit, profit margin, growth, regional performance, product categories, and customer sales.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance and helps identify important sales and profitability trends.

Users can interact with different filters such as **Region, Segment, and Category** to dynamically analyze the data.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance
* Track total profit and profit margin
* Monitor business growth
* Compare performance across regions
* Analyze profit by region
* Analyze profit margin
* Identify the Top 10 customers by sales
* Provide interactive business insights

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

The dashboard displays important business KPIs:

* Average Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Growth
* Top Performing Region

### 📍 Region Analysis

Analyze sales and profitability across different geographical regions.

### 👥 Segment Analysis

Interactive segment filters allow users to analyze performance for different customer segments.

### 📦 Category Analysis

The dashboard provides category-level analysis for understanding product performance.

### 💰 Profit Analysis

Visualizations are used to analyze:

* Profit by Region
* Profit by Margin
* Overall profitability

### 🏆 Customer Analysis

The dashboard identifies the **Top 10 Customers by Sales**, helping businesses understand their highest-value customers.
